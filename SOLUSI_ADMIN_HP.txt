=============================================
  TROUBLESHOOTING
=============================================

Q: "Tidak terautentikasi"
A: Belum login! Akses /admin/login dulu

Q: "Email atau password salah"
A: Check credentials atau buat admin baru

Q: "404 Not Found" at /admin/login
A: Routes not loaded
   php artisan route:list | findstr admin
   php artisan config:clear

Q: "This site can't be reached"
A: Firewall blocking atau server not running
   Run: BUKA_FIREWALL.bat (as Admin)

Q: Blank page after login
A: Check browser console (F12)
   Check Laravel logs: storage/logs/laravel.log

Q: Redirect loop
A: Clear session & cookies
   php artisan session:flush
   Browser: Clear cookies for localhost
